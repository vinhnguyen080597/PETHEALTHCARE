Nội quy Marketplace (Tiếng Việt)

Pet Health Care chỉ là nền tảng kết nối — không phải bên bán, không giữ tiền, không bảo lãnh sức khỏe thú, giấy tờ hay giao dịch. Người đăng tin chịu trách nhiệm về nội dung và tuân thủ pháp luật Việt Nam.
  • Loài được phép: chó, mèo, chim, cá, chuột, bò, heo, gà và thú cưng hợp pháp khác — chỉ khi việc nuôi, buôn bán hoặc chuyển giao tuân thủ luật địa phương.
  • Cấm: động vật hoang dã, loài nguy cấp, quý hiếm (CITES), buôn bán trái phép, nội dung ngược đãi, lừa đảo, thông tin y tế/sức khỏe gây hiểu nhầm.
  • Tin đăng: ảnh/video thật, mô tả trung thực; không cam kết “100% khỏe” nếu chưa có xác nhận thú y.
  • Giao dịch: mọi thỏa thuận, chuyển tiền, giao nhận diễn ra ngoài app — các bên tự chịu trách nhiệm. Nên gặp trực tiếp hoặc kiểm thú y trước khi nhận bé.
  • Báo cáo: dùng nút báo cáo trong app khi thấy tin đáng ngờ. Admin có thể gỡ tin hoặc khóa tài khoản vi phạm.

1. Platform Role

Pet Health Care provides Pet Feed as a discovery and contact tool. We display listings, comments, and messages submitted by users. We are not the seller, broker, payment processor, transporter, or guarantor of any animal, health claim, pedigree, price, or transaction outcome.

Unless a future feature explicitly says otherwise, the app does not hold deposits, process pet purchase payments, or provide escrow, shipping, or ownership transfer documents.

2. Who May Post

Users with permission to create Pet Feed listings (including verified breeders and authorized admin accounts) may publish listings. You must use your own account, provide accurate contact details you control, and accept full responsibility for what you publish.

3. Allowed Animals and Listings

Pet Feed may include listings for common companion and farm-related animals when lawful, such as:

  • Dogs and cats
  • Birds, fish, and small rodents kept as pets
  • Cattle, pigs, chickens, and similar animals when local law allows the transfer you describe

You are responsible for confirming that the species, age, location, and transfer method comply with applicable laws and regulations where the listing is offered and where the animal will go.

4. Prohibited Listings and Content

You must not post listings or related content that:

  • Involve wild, protected, endangered, or illegally traded animals, including CITES-regulated species
  • Promote poaching, smuggling, cruelty, neglect, or unsafe handling
  • Are false, misleading, fraudulent, or impersonate another person or business
  • Make unsupported absolute health, vaccination, pedigree, or “guaranteed healthy” claims
  • Include abusive, hateful, harassing, graphic, or illegal material
  • Expose private personal data you do not have permission to share
  • Contain spam, duplicate scam posts, malware, phishing, or attempts to bypass moderation

5. Listing Quality and Honesty

Good listings help buyers make informed decisions. We expect:

  • Clear, recent photos and a short video of the actual animal offered
  • Honest descriptions of age, breed or type, location, price note, vaccine/deworming status, and paperwork
  • Transparent adoption or transfer conditions without hidden fees or pressure tactics
  • No absolute medical promises unless supported by appropriate documentation and professional context

6. Transactions and Safety

Any payment, deposit, contract, delivery, or ownership transfer happens directly between users, outside Pet Health Care. Be cautious with upfront transfers to unknown contacts. Meet in safe public places when possible and consider a veterinary check before finalizing a transfer.

Pet Health Care does not verify every listing, identity, document, or health statement. Breeder verification or admin approval in the app is a moderation step, not a warranty or endorsement.

7. Comments, Chat, and Notifications

Comments and direct messages must follow the same respect, honesty, and legality standards as listings. Do not use chat to harass users, solicit unsafe payments, or share prohibited content.

8. Reporting Violations

Users should report suspicious, unsafe, or policy-violating listings using the in-app report feature. Include helpful context when possible. We review reports and may remove, archive, restrict, or reject content and accounts when we believe there is a safety, fraud, legal, or policy concern.

9. Moderation and Enforcement

We may, at our discretion:

  • Require admin review before a listing is published
  • Remove or archive listings that violate these guidelines or our Terms
  • Suspend or terminate posting privileges or accounts
  • Preserve records needed for safety, fraud prevention, or legal compliance

We are not obligated to monitor every message or explain every moderation decision.

10. Your Acknowledgment in the App

Before submitting a listing for review or publication, the app may require you to confirm that your post is lawful, does not involve wild or illegally traded animals, is truthful, and that you accept responsibility for the content and any related transaction.

11. Changes

We may update these Marketplace Guidelines as Pet Feed evolves. Continued use of marketplace features after updates means you accept the revised guidelines, together with our Terms of Service.
